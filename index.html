    The rules, all of them

    • A million shares a season, one gold each, first come first served.
    • One-way. The gold is burned. Nothing pays it back — no redemption, no dividend, not this season.
    • From the seal only. Shares are bought with sealed gold: a signed trail move, carried home and redeemed at Tideholm like any other slip. They appear in the book when the slip is redeemed.

    • There is a prize at the end of the season, in testnet sats, shared out by hand in proportion to the shares held when the tide turns. How much depends on the till; the book is the only record that counts.
